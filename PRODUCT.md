# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: owners of premium, performance and exotic vehicles who want work
done on their car (wraps, paint protection, coating, tint). Their job on the
site: be convinced by the quality of the work, then request a quote or
booking.

Secondary audiences (for example Instagram followers browsing builds) haven't
been confirmed as a target; serve them only through the primary journey.

## Product Purpose

The Mansour Customs website presents the shop's work and turns interested
vehicle owners into quote requests. The current deliverable is a
presentation-ready homepage demo built by theDiablo to pitch the full site.
Success means the client sees an exclusive, premium experience worthy of
their brand, with a clear path from admiration to enquiry.

## Positioning

Social reach and visible work are the proof. Mansour Customs has 270k+
Instagram followers and a public body of builds. The work speaks for itself,
so the site leads with real vehicles and finished results rather than claims.

## Operating Context

- *Inferred, not confirmed:* with an Instagram-led audience, many visitors
  likely arrive on mobile from Instagram, so the mobile experience is
  treated as primary.
- Evaluation is visual: owners compare finish quality, materials and builds
  before contacting the shop.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed services:
- Vinyl wraps / colour change
- PPF (paint protection film)
- Ceramic coating
- Window tint

Undecided (don't invent): shop location, service area, contact details,
social URLs, pricing, hours, booking method, final service names and
descriptions, and whether individual service pages will exist (the demo
shows "Coming Soon").

## Product Principles

1. Show, don't claim: real vehicles and real finishes carry the persuasion.
2. Every path leads to an enquiry; admiration should convert to a quote
   request.
3. Mobile-first: design the phone experience as deliberately as desktop.
4. Truth over polish: placeholders stay clearly marked until the client
   confirms the facts.
